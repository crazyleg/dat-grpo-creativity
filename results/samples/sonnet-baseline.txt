**The Unyielding Flame**

Beneath the shifting stars and fleeting tide,
Where time erodes the stone and fades the name,
Our bond remains, a quiet, sacred guide,
A flame that burns without consuming flame.

The seasons turn from winter’s bitter frost
To summer’s heat, yet neither war nor peace
Can break the vow that we have sworn and lost
To nothing but the truth of what we please.

Let empires rise and crumble into dust,
Let memories blur like smoke upon the wind,
In you, I find the truth I trust,
A heart that beats for yours, to the end.

So let the ages pass in endless flight,
My love for you shall outlast the light.
