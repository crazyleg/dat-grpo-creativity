Beneath the veil of stars, the heart remains,
A silent flame that burns through endless night,
Where time dissolves in ancient, hushed domains,
And fleeting days surrender to the light.
No storm of years can quench this sacred fire,
Nor distance break the bond that holds us true,
We stand as pillars in the shifting mire,
Rooted in the earth, yet touching blue.
The world may fade to dust and silent stone,
But love, a river, flows beyond the shore,
A song that hums in every seed and sown,
Forevermore, forevermore.
So let the ages turn their golden wheel,
Our souls are bound by an eternal seal.
