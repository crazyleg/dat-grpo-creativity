The river speaks in spirals, soft and low,
"Come, let the current take you where you go.
I am the hour, the minute, the second’s breath,
A living pulse beneath the surface’s death.
You are the anchor, the unmoving ground,
The silence where my restless voice is drowned.
I carve your edges with a patient hand,
And wear the shape of years across the land.
You think you stand, but I have worn you thin,
My motion is the clock that you live in.
So listen, stone, to what the water knows:
You are not fixed, you are the river’s flow.
The solid self is just a moment’s pause,
A single ripple in the endless cause.
I carry the sky, the star, the falling leaf,
And time is only what I mean to keep."
