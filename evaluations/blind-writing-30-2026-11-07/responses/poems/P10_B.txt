The sky sheds its heavy grey,
A hush falls on the sleeping street.
No footprints yet, no rustling leaves,
Just a slow, soft descent of light.
The world wears a new, silent coat,
Erasing the lines of the old.
A single feather lands on the stone,
Then another, and another still.
The air tastes of nothing, clean,
The earth holds its breath in stillness.
Dark branches wear a crown of lace,
And the sun, a pale coin, fades.
No sound but the settling dust,
A quiet, endless, gentle rain
Of light that never touches the ground
Until it has covered it all.
