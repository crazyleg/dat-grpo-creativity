The current speaks in rushing tongues,
Of seconds lost and moments found,
While silence holds the ancient stone,
A heavy, unmoving ground.

"You flow," the rock replies, "yet I remain,
A witness to your endless race.
You carve my face with softest rain,
But time is not a single place."

The water laughs, a bubbling sound,
That echoes off the jagged wall.
"I am the change, you are the ground,
Together, we answer all."

For in the wear, the truth is seen:
The stone is time, the river’s dream.
