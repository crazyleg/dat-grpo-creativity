The paper yellowed, soft as dried leaves,
Held by a clip that rusted through the years.
Her ink, a faded violet, curves and dives,
Across the flour and sugar, through the tears.
The 'g' descends like a vine in autumn,
The 't' is crossed with a steady hand.
No digital font could capture this,
The slight tremor of a loving land.
She measured love in cups and spoons,
In notes that say "add more if you please."
The coffee ring stains mark the time,
When she would sit and talk with ease.
Now I trace the letters with my thumb,
And smell the ghost of cinnamon and rose.
A map to meals I cannot quite recreate,
But hold the warmth that she bestowed.
