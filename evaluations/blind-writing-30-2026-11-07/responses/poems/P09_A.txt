I trace the digits in the dark,
A ghost of light, a phantom spark.
No ring, no buzz, no breathing sound,
Just silence where the voices bound.

The memory holds the shape of you,
A map I memorized, now blue.
I dial the air, a hollow prayer,
And find no one is waiting there.

The screen goes black, the connection dead,
With nothing left for words to spread.
I keep the code, a silent key,
To lock the door of who you were to me.
