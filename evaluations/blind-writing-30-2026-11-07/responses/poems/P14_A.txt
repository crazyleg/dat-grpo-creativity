The ink has faded, blue to rust,
a loop of "s" like a sleeping bird.
"Two eggs," she wrote, then paused,
and crossed out "fresh" with a sigh.
The margin holds a coffee ring,
a ghost of Tuesday, 1984.
I trace the cursive with my thumb,
the paper thin as winter skin.
Her "g" hangs low, a question mark,
her "r" sharp as a thorn.
The recipe is just a map
to a kitchen that no longer stands,
a door left open,
smelling of yeast and rain.
