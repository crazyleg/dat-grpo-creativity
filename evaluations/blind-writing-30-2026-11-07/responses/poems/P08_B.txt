The ink has faded into ghostly gray,
Where rivers once carved their winding way.
The names are blurred, the borders soft and thin,
A world that was, now lost within the bin.
No footfalls echo on the dusty street,
No bells ring out beneath the ancient seat.
Just paper thin, and brittle to the touch,
Holding the breath of a vanished clutch.
The compass points to nothing but the void,
A story of the past, now strictly avoided.
We trace the lines with trembling, careful hands,
In memory’s cold and silent lands.
The place is gone, the air is still and dead,
A map of where the living ended.
