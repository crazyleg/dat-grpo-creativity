I was forged for the turning,
A promise of open doors,
But the tumblers have shifted,
And the cylinders ignore
My precise, toothed geometry.
I am a ghost of access,
A key without a kingdom,
Locked out of my own ending.

I rust in the pocket’s dark,
A useless metal tongue,
No longer the master’s hand,
No longer the song
Of the latch giving way.
I am just weight, and shape,
A memory of escape,
That no one will ever take.
