I carried the word across the sea,
a fragile bird in a paper cage.
Did it sing the same in your ear?
Or did the salt air change its song?

I stitched the silence into thread,
but the needle slipped the heart of it.
You nod, you smile, you understand—
yet I am lost in the looking glass.

The ghost of the original hums
a frequency I cannot hold.
I offer you a shadow, shaped
by the light of my own doubt.

Is this the meaning, or the echo?
I cannot tell the two apart.
The bridge is built, the ferry waits,
but the shore remains uncharted.
