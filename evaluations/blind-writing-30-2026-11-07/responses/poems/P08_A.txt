The ink has faded where the river bent,
A blue vein dried upon the parchment’s skin.
Here stood the market, now a blank descent
Of white, where shadows of the living thin.
The compass rose points north to empty air,
No harbor bell rings out across the bay.
The streets are ghosts, suspended in despair,
A grid of lines that lead to yesterday.
The town is gone, yet here the outline stays,
A wound that time has failed to heal.
We trace the borders of its empty days,
And find the world is less than what we feel.
The legend notes the ruins, small and gray,
A footnote in the atlas of the dead.
We keep the map, though there is nothing there,
A promise that the lost were understood.
