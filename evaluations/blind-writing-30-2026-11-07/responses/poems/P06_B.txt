I was forged for the turning, the snap, the release,
a promise of passage, a promise of home.
Now I am a ghost in the brass of the keyhole,
a shape without a shadow, a name without a sound.
The wards have shifted, the doors have grown old,
and I am the only thing left that is new.
I hang from a ring with the other dead keys,
a chorus of rust and forgotten names.
I do not fit. I do not open.
I am the question no one asks anymore,
the answer that lost its question,
the lock that forgot how to close.
I am the space between the teeth,
the silence after the last word,
the door that is always ajar,
and never, ever, opens.
