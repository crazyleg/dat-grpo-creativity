The clock ticks slow, a wooden heartbeat,
While shadows stretch across the floor.
The pipes sigh deep in hidden walls,
Releasing steam from long before.
A floorboard groans beneath the weight
Of dust that settles, soft and still,
The wind taps glass with gentle fingers,
A whisper against the window sill.
The furnace hums a low refrain,
To keep the cold at bay,
While silence swallows every sound,
And night holds fast its sway.
No footfalls echo on the tile,
No voices break the air,
Just the house breathing, alive and alone,
Suspended in the dark.
