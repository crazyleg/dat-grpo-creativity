The sky releases its silent breath,
A thousand petals drift from above.
The world is wrapped in quiet soft,
Where shadows lose their sharp edges.
The trees wear veils of spun silk,
And silence swallows every sound.
The ground is paved with diamond dust,
A crisp and crystalline descent.
No footprints mark the pristine field,
Just the hush of falling light.
The air tastes sharp and clean,
A frozen stillness holds the day.
We watch the gray dissolve into
A landscape of pure, bright peace.
