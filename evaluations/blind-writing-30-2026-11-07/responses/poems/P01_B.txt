The brass wheel groans beneath my palm,
A final turn against the dark.
I watch the beam sweep through the storm,
No longer needing my own spark.

The oil is dry, the wick is cold,
The silence swallows every sound.
I hold the key to the iron door,
Where the electric pulse is found.

No more to climb the spiral stair,
No more to fight the rising tide.
The machine will wake the sleeping sea,
With no one left to stay inside.

I step back from the glassy eye,
And let the current take its course.
The light remains, but I am gone,
Lost in the vast and empty force.
