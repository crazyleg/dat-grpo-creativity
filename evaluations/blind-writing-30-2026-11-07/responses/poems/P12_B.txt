The velvet seats sit silent, still,
Holding ghosts of thunder’s will.
Dust motes dance in fading light,
Chasing echoes of the night.
On the stage, the rosin dust
Lies in patterns, soft and just.
A cello leans against the wall,
Hearing the silence answer all.
The air remains thick with song,
Where the strings had stretched so long.
Now only the clock ticks slow,
Marking time’s inevitable flow.
The door creaks shut with final breath,
Sealing the magic in its death.
Yet the music lingers, deep and true,
In the quiet, empty room.
