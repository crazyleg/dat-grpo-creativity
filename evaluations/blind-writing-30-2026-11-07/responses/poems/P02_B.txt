**To "Thou"**

I found you gathering dust in the margins,
A ghost of grammar, soft and thin,
Once the sharp edge of a lover’s warning,
Now a whisper, lost within.

You were the "you" before it softened,
A blade of intimacy, precise,
A bond that wasn’t merely bonded,
But cut with a specific vice.

I try to speak you, feel the archaic weight,
The way the tongue must curl to say,
A bridge to a world where love was fate,
And words were built to last, not fray.

So rest now, in the pages of the old,
Where "thee" and "thou" still hold their ground,
A story of a voice grown cold,
Before the modern tongue was found.
