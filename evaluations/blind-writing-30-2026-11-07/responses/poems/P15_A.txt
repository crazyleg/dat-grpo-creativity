The clock ticks loud against the wall,
A pendulum swinging in a hollow hall.
I trace the grain of the wooden chair,
Breathing shallow air, suspended in fear.
No name is spoken, no label affixed,
Just a silence thick, like smoke or mist.
The doctor’s pen hovers, a black eclipse,
Over the page where the truth persists.
My heart beats a drum on a silent stage,
Counting seconds, turning the page.
Is it the end, or just a pause?
The verdict hangs in the empty space.
I hold my breath, a frozen bird,
Waiting for the word, yet un-unsaid.
The paper slides, a white surrender,
And the world holds its breath, no longer.
