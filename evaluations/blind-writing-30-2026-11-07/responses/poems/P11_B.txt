I held the ghost in my bare hands,
A shape of light, a fading sound,
And tried to catch what time demands
Before the silence swallowed ground.

The word was heavy, thick with dust,
A key that fit no modern door;
I turned it, watched it rust and rust,
And felt the original core.

Did I keep the soul or just the skin?
Did I translate the heat or chill?
The bridge is built, but where I’ve been,
The echo has no way to fill.

I speak the truth in borrowed tongue,
A shadow cast by someone’s face,
And wonder if the meaning sung
Still holds its place in time and space.
