The velvet seats hold ghosts of breath,
A hush that swallows every chord.
Dust motes dance in the fading light,
Like notes suspended, lost, and lost.
The stage, a bare and wooden stage,
Still hums with the echo of the crowd,
A ghostly applause, a phantom roar,
That fades into the velvet shroud.
The conductor’s baton lies forgotten,
A silent wand of wood and wire,
While silence, vast and cold, descends,
To claim the empty, aching air.
The doors creak shut, one by one,
Sealing the music in the dark,
Leaving only the smell of dust
And the memory of the spark.
